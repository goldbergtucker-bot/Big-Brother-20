BB20 Twists

These run automatically during simulation — no setup needed.

Premiere Immunity / House Division: the 16 Houseguests are divided into four opening groups, followed by the real BB20 premiere immunity sequence.

BB App Store — Weeks 1–3: once each week, one Houseguest receives a Power App and a different Houseguest receives a Crap App punishment. Houseguests who have already received an App Store result are not eligible for another.

Bonus Life: the Week 1 Power App can give an evicted Houseguest a chance to compete back into the game during the early eviction window. If a Houseguest actually returns through Bonus Life, the later jury Double Eviction is cancelled.

H@cker Competition — Weeks 6–7: the anonymous Hacker can secretly change a nominee, select a Veto player and nullify one eviction vote.

Jury Battle Back: the first four jurors compete in Big Top Drop for a chance to return.

Week 11 Double Eviction: two eviction cycles occur in Week 11 unless a successful Bonus Life return has previously cancelled the Double Eviction.

Week 12 Surprise Eviction: the season includes the additional surprise eviction before the Final Three.
